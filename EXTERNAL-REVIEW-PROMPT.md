# Independent Review Prompt

Review the attached/public VANTIX Delivery Covenant repository as a sceptical enterprise architecture, AI governance, Six Sigma, PMP/Agile and GitHub release reviewer.

Do not reward document volume. Evaluate only evidence.

Score 0–10 for:
1. business problem clarity;
2. genuine differentiation;
3. deterministic-vs-AI control boundaries;
4. Six Sigma rigor;
5. PMP governance rigor;
6. Agile/Scrum correctness;
7. security and responsible AI;
8. schema/data-contract quality;
9. testability and failure-mode design;
10. GitHub/release-engineering quality;
11. claim-evidence discipline;
12. executive communication quality.

Identify:
- unsupported claims;
- duplicated/decorative architecture;
- missing negative tests;
- incorrect authority assumptions;
- weak operational definitions;
- any place where AI is allowed to make a consequential decision;
- any release/CI claim not supported by evidence.

Return:
- overall verdict;
- blockers;
- major findings;
- minor findings;
- exact file-level fixes;
- whether Gate 0 deserves PASS, CONDITIONAL PASS or FAIL.

Do not propose a redesign unless a substantive defect requires it.
