# VOC-to-CTQ Tree

## Voice of Customer: “Do not tell me the project is green if it is drifting from what we approved.”

CTQs:
- material changes approved before implementation;
- drift detected before milestone failure;
- decision authority verified;
- milestone acceptance backed by evidence;
- benefit claims backed by measurements;
- stale evidence identified;
- contradictory statuses preserved;
- stories traceable to approved outcomes.

No Sigma-level or capability claim is authorised at Gate 0.
