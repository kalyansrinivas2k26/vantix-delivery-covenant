# CTQ Definitions v1

| CTQ | Unit | Defect | Opportunity | Candidate measure |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Material change approval integrity | One material change | Implemented before required approval | 1 | % approved before implementation |
| Milestone evidence integrity | One milestone assessment | Accepted with incomplete required evidence | 1 | % complete evidence |
| Benefit evidence integrity | One benefit claim | Achieved/sustained without measured outcome evidence | 1 | % evidence-backed claims |
| Decision authority integrity | One consequential decision | Decision by insufficient authority | 1 | unauthorised decision rate |
| Micro-change escape | One configured change window | Aggregate materiality crossed without escalation | 1 | escape rate |
| Evidence freshness | One required evidence item | Evidence exceeds configured freshness window | 1 | stale-evidence rate |
| Outcome traceability | One story/feature requiring linkage | No approved outcome link | 1 | unlinked-story rate |

No Sigma level, DPMO, Cpk or Ppk claim is authorised yet.
