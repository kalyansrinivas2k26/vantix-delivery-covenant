# CTQ Definitions v1

| CTQ | Unit | Defect | Opportunity |
|---|---|---|---|
| Material change approval integrity | One material change | Implemented before required approval | 1 |
| Milestone evidence integrity | One milestone assessment | Accepted with incomplete evidence | 1 |
| Benefit evidence integrity | One benefit claim | Achieved/sustained without measured evidence | 1 |
| Decision authority integrity | One consequential decision | Decision by insufficient authority | 1 |
| Micro-change escape | One configured change window | Aggregate materiality crossed without escalation | 1 |
| Evidence freshness | One required evidence item | Evidence exceeds freshness window | 1 |
| Outcome traceability | One story/feature requiring linkage | No approved outcome link | 1 |

No Sigma level, DPMO, Cpk or Ppk claim is authorised at Gate 1.
