# Change Control Plan — Starter

## Purpose
Prevent individually small or collectively material changes from altering the authorised project covenant without the required decision authority.

## Change classes
- clarification within approved boundary;
- isolated minor change;
- repeated minor change;
- cumulative material change;
- formal baseline change;
- emergency exception;
- insufficient evidence.

## Minimum record
Each change must retain a change ID, project ID, observed time, description, affected commitment, evidence IDs, materiality result, required authority, approval status and resulting baseline/policy version where applicable.

## Decision rule
Materiality and authority are deterministic configuration outcomes. AI may explain the evidence but may not approve the change.

## Gate 0 limitation
No organisation-specific materiality threshold is asserted. The included policy values are synthetic portfolio defaults pending owner validation.
