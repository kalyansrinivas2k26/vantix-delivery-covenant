# Risk Model

Probability and impact are configured deterministic values, not LLM guesses.

Synthetic portfolio scale:
- Probability: 1–5
- Impact: 1–5
- Exposure = Probability × Impact

Classification:
- 1–4 Low
- 5–9 Moderate
- 10–15 High
- 16–25 Critical

Risk acceptance requires the configured Risk Owner. AI may explain exposure but cannot assign probability/impact or accept risk.
