# Limitations

This repository currently represents a synthetic portfolio build.

- No real customer data is included.
- No live Salesforce organisation is required for Gate 0.
- No live n8n workflow evidence is claimed.
- No live AI-provider evidence is claimed.
- Included policy thresholds are synthetic defaults pending owner validation.
- Six Sigma capability metrics must not be interpreted as real organisational performance until population, sampling boundary, process stability and measurement-system fitness are established.
- The project does not claim external certification, regulatory compliance, production readiness, enterprise proof or third-party endorsement.
