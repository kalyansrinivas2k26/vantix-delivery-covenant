# VANTIX Delivery Covenant
## Adaptive Delivery Governance & Benefits Assurance

**Prevents Agile delivery activity from silently diverging from approved project intent, decision authority and expected business outcomes.**

> From sprint activity to verified project outcomes—with evidence, decision rights and human control.

## What this project is

VANTIX Delivery Covenant is an evidence-governed reconciliation layer between Agile execution and project authority. It compares delivery reality with approved governance commitments, preserves contradictory indicators, validates decision rights, checks milestone evidence, and separates delivered outputs from evidenced business outcomes.

This repository is designed as a portfolio-grade reference implementation. It uses synthetic data only until live-provider validation is explicitly performed and evidenced.

## Core design

`Delivery Reality Ledger` + `Governance Commitment Ledger` → deterministic controls → bounded AI interpretation (optional) → human governance gate → traceable decision envelope.

Consequential decisions remain with explicitly identified human authorities.

## What makes it different

The project is not a task manager, Scrum board, sprint summariser, Jira replacement, Salesforce replacement, or PPM replacement. Its control focus is the gap between healthy-looking execution and authorised project intent.

Key controls:
- commitment drift detection;
- cumulative micro-change detection;
- decision-rights validation;
- milestone evidence assurance;
- benefits assurance;
- contradiction preservation;
- evidence-traceable executive decision support.

## Assurance disciplines

- **Six Sigma:** candidate CTQs, operational definitions, FMEA, defect taxonomy and a starter control plan, with capability claims explicitly deferred until measurement prerequisites are satisfied.
- **PMP / project governance:** charter, RAID/Risk, RACI, starter change/configuration control, decision rights and benefits-management artefacts, with organisation-specific thresholds still pending validation.
- **Agile / Scrum:** outcome traceability, Definition of Ready/Done, sprint-goal alignment, team estimation boundaries and Product Owner authority boundaries.
- **Responsible AI:** deterministic-first, bounded AI, validated outputs, fail-closed routing and human approval.
