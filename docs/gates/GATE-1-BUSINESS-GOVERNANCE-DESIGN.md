# Gate 1 — Business & Governance Design

## Objective
Convert the Gate 0 concept into an explicit, testable governance design before any workflow implementation.

## Decision supported
**Is current Agile execution still aligned with authorised project intent, decision authority and expected benefits—and what human decision is required if it is not?**

## Gate 1 outputs
- approved project charter baseline;
- outcome map;
- two-ledger operating model;
- decision-rights matrix;
- drift taxonomy;
- cumulative micro-change model;
- milestone evidence rules;
- benefit evidence rules;
- CTQ definitions;
- risk model;
- deterministic policy catalogue;
- Gate 1 acceptance checklist.

## Guardrail
No AI or n8n implementation may redefine an accepted Gate 1 policy unless a substantive defect is discovered and recorded through change control.
