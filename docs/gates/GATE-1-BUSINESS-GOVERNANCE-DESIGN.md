# Gate 1 — Business & Governance Design

## Objective
Convert the Gate 0 concept into an explicit, testable governance design before workflow implementation.

## Primary decision
**Is current Agile execution still aligned with authorised project intent, decision authority and expected benefits—and what human decision is required if it is not?**

## Outputs
- project charter baseline;
- outcome map;
- two-ledger operating model;
- decision-rights matrix;
- drift taxonomy;
- cumulative micro-change model;
- milestone evidence rules;
- benefit evidence rules;
- CTQ definitions;
- risk model;
- deterministic policy catalogue.

## Design freeze rule
After Gate 1 acceptance, later gates may implement these controls but must not silently redefine them. Any substantive policy change requires a recorded governance change.
