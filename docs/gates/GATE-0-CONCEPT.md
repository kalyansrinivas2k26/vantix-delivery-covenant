# Gate 0 — Project Concept

## One-page concept

VANTIX Delivery Covenant addresses a common governance blind spot: a project can look healthy at sprint level while silently diverging from authorised business outcomes, scope boundaries, risk appetite, milestone obligations, decision rights or benefit expectations.

The solution establishes two versioned truth ledgers: a **Delivery Reality Ledger** for Agile execution evidence and a **Governance Commitment Ledger** for approved project commitments. Deterministic controls reconcile them before any AI interpretation occurs.

The control engine evaluates commitment drift, cumulative micro-change, decision authority, milestone evidence, benefits evidence and contradictory indicators. Bounded AI may interpret evidence, challenge contradictions and draft recommendations, but consequential decisions remain with named human authorities.

The intended result is not another status dashboard. It is a traceable decision-support layer that makes invisible governance divergence visible before it becomes an avoidable programme failure.

- Product Owner
- ScrumMaster / Agile Delivery Lead
- PMO / Governance Lead
- Sponsor
- Risk Owner
- Benefit Owner
- Delivery Team
- Internal Audit / Assurance reviewer

## Five enterprise failure scenarios

1. **Green sprint / red project:** Velocity and completion remain healthy while delivered stories no longer trace to the approved outcome.
2. **Micro-change accumulation:** Several individually minor changes collectively exceed a material scope or contingency threshold.
3. **Authority breach:** A decision is recorded as approved by a person who does not hold the required authority.
4. **False milestone completion:** Work items are Done, but acceptance evidence, dependency closure or authorised business acceptance is missing.
5. **Output mistaken for benefit:** A dashboard or automation is delivered and reported as a successful benefit although the intended operational outcome has not been measured.

## Hypothesis

If delivery evidence is continuously reconciled against versioned project commitments using deterministic governance controls, then material divergence can be surfaced earlier and escalated to the correct human authority with stronger evidence traceability.

## Value proposition

Delivery Covenant helps leaders distinguish **activity from authorised progress** and **output from evidenced outcome**.

## Business problem statement

Agile execution evidence can remain locally healthy while the authorised project covenant has materially changed, creating a governance blind spot between delivery activity and approved business intent.

## Gate 0 position

The enterprise problem and control architecture are sufficiently defined for structured design review. Organisation-specific authorities and thresholds remain intentionally unconfirmed.

## Confirmed

- The project purpose is evidence-governed reconciliation between Agile delivery evidence and authorised project governance commitments.
- Consequential decisions remain with explicitly identified human authorities.
- Deterministic controls precede optional AI interpretation for governance-sensitive decisions.
- Contradictory indicators must remain visible rather than being averaged into one status.
- The public portfolio build uses synthetic data until live evidence is explicitly introduced and sanitised.

## Assumed

- Stable evidence identifiers can be produced by source systems or the canonical ingestion layer.
- Authority roles can be represented as versioned policy configuration.
- Synthetic materiality, freshness and confidence thresholds may be used for portfolio testing if clearly labelled.
- Benefit evidence can be represented independently from delivery completion evidence.

## Must be validated

- organisation-specific materiality thresholds;
- contingency and financial approval boundaries;
- risk acceptance authorities;
- milestone acceptance authorities;
- benefit ownership and acceptance authority;
- evidence-freshness expectations;
- any external milestone or programme-specific governance rules.

## Evidence needed before Gate 1

An owner-approved Gate 0 disposition confirming either:
1. the real organisation-specific thresholds and authorities to use; or
2. explicit approval to continue the portfolio build using clearly labelled synthetic defaults.

Gate 1 must not represent synthetic defaults as real organisational policy.
