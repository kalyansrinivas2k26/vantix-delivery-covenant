# Executive One-Pager — VANTIX Delivery Covenant

## Thesis
Healthy Agile activity does not prove that a project remains aligned to its authorised outcome, governance boundaries or expected benefits.

## Problem
Projects can report green velocity, completed stories and on-time releases while accumulating unapproved scope, crossing decision-right boundaries, accepting milestones without sufficient evidence or claiming outputs as realised benefits.

## Control proposition
Delivery Covenant reconciles two versioned evidence sets:
1. **Delivery Reality** — what Agile execution shows happened.
2. **Governance Commitment** — what the project was authorised to deliver and who may decide what.

Deterministic controls identify material differences before optional bounded AI is allowed to interpret them.

## Decision supported
**Is current Agile execution still aligned with authorised project intent, decision authority and expected benefits—and what human decision is required if it is not?**

## Why it matters
The design makes contradictions visible rather than averaging them into a single reassuring RAG status. A project can therefore remain Green for delivery while Red for governance alignment, with the disagreement explicitly escalated.

## Controls
- commitment drift;
- cumulative micro-change;
- decision-right validation;
- milestone evidence assurance;
- benefits evidence assurance;
- contradiction preservation;
- human governance gate;
- versioned audit trail.
