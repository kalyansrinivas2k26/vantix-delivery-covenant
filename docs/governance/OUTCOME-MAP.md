# Outcome Map

## North-star outcome
Agile execution remains demonstrably aligned with authorised project outcomes and governance boundaries.

## Outcome chain
1. Delivery evidence is traceable.
2. Material drift is detected before governance surprise.
3. Decision authority is respected.
4. Milestones are evidence-backed.
5. Benefits are measured rather than assumed.
6. Contradictory indicators remain visible.
7. Human decisions are auditable.
