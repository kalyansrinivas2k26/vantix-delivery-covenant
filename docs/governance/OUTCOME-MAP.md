# Outcome Map

## North-star outcome
Agile delivery remains demonstrably aligned with authorised project outcomes and governance boundaries.

## Outcome chain
1. Delivery evidence is traceable.
2. Drift is detected before governance surprise.
3. Decision authority is respected.
4. Milestones are evidence-backed.
5. Benefits are measured, not assumed.
6. Contradictions remain visible.
7. Human decisions are auditable.
