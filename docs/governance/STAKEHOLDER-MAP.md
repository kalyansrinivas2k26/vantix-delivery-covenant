# Stakeholder Map

| Stakeholder | Primary concern | Decision / contribution |
|---|---|---|
| Sponsor | Outcome, funding, material change | Scope/rebaseline/closure authority as configured |
| Project/Programme Manager | Integrated delivery/governance health | Coordinates evidence and escalation |
| Product Owner | Product value and backlog | Prioritisation within approved boundaries |
| Delivery Team | Feasible sprint delivery | Estimation and sprint commitment |
| ScrumMaster / Agile Lead | Flow and impediments | Facilitates delivery; does not override governance |
| PMO / Governance Lead | Baselines and controls | Governance policy and escalation |
| Risk Owner | Accepted exposure | Risk acceptance |
| Business/Milestone Owner | Acceptance evidence | Milestone acceptance |
| Benefit Owner | Outcome evidence | Benefit acceptance |
| Assurance / Audit | Traceability | Independent evidence review |
